ACOG Prenatal Care Roadmap (Weeks 6–42)

Standard obstetric visit cadence: Every 4 weeks (Weeks 4–28), Every 2 weeks (Weeks 28–36), and Weekly (Weeks 36–41), plus twice-weekly late-term NST/BPP surveillance at Week 41+. Check off visits as you complete them.

Obstetric Symptom Triage: When to Call OB vs. Go to L&D Immediately

Distinguishing normal maternal physiological adaptations from urgent obstetric warning signs. When in doubt, Labor & Delivery triage and your on-call OB/Midwife prefer you call early.

1. Immediate L&D / Emergency (Red)

Proceed directly to Labor & Delivery Triage (if ≥20 weeks) or the nearest Emergency Department. Do not wait for a callback.

2. Same-Day OB Call Within 1–4 Hours (Amber)

Page your OB/Midwife on-call line today for targeted lab work, fetal monitoring, or prescription treatment.

3. Physiological Adaptation (Green)

Expected hormonal or biomechanical changes of pregnancy that can be managed safely at home and discussed at your next scheduled visit.
